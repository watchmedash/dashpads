Privacy Policy

Last updated July 21, 2026

Overview

DashPad is a local, offline application. This policy explains what that means for your data.

Data we collect

DashPad does not collect any personal data. There's no account, no sign-up, and no analytics or tracking of any kind built into the app.

Where your data is stored

Your snippets are stored locally in your device's app data folder. They are never transmitted anywhere, and DashPad does not communicate with any remote server.

Third parties

DashPad does not sell or share your data with third parties, because it doesn't collect any to begin with. Purchases are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store, subject to Microsoft's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

DashPad is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age, since it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of DashPad after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@dashpad.top.
