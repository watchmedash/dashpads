About DashPad

DashPad is a simple snippet manager for Windows. Save short pieces of text you use often, like notes, templates, addresses, or anything you find yourself pasting again and again, and get them back on your clipboard in one click.

There's no account to create and no internet connection required. Everything you save stays on your own device.

Why DashPad

Most people retype or hunt down the same handful of snippets over and over: a reply template, a command they always forget, an address, a signature. DashPad keeps them all in one place, organized with tags, and ready to paste with a single click.

How it works

Add a snippet with a label and optional tags, then click it any time you need it and it's copied to your clipboard automatically. You can also paste directly (Ctrl+V) to save clipboard text as a new snippet without opening any dialog.

Pricing

DashPad is sold as a one-time purchase through the Microsoft Store. There are no subscriptions, ads, or in-app purchases.
